# Meridian Analytics - Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2026

## Standard refunds

Customers on a monthly plan may request a full refund within **14 days** of the
initial charge. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically
settle within 5 to 7 business days.

Annual plans are refundable on a pro-rata basis for the unused portion of the
term, minus a 10% administrative fee. Requests must be submitted before the
tenth month of the term.

## What is not refundable

- Usage-based overage charges that have already been billed
- Professional services and onboarding fees once delivery has started
- Any plan where the account has been suspended for violating the acceptable
  use policy

## How to request a refund

Email billing@meridian-analytics.example with the account ID and the invoice
number. Requests are reviewed within two business days. Approved refunds are
processed on the next billing cycle run, which happens every Tuesday.

## Disputes

If a refund request is denied, customers may escalate once to the billing
manager. Escalations are answered within five business days. Meridian does not
offer refunds for disputes raised more than 90 days after the original charge.
